Frequently Asked Questions

What is the duration of the AI Powered Digital Marketing Course?

We offer the AI Diploma Program in Digital Marketing Intelligence (DMI) with a diploma from a UGC approved university, plus three Certification Programs: Master in Digital Marketing (MIDM) with 500+ hours of practical training and a 2-month internship, Advanced Digital Marketing (AIDM) with 140+ hours of training, and Professional in Digital Marketing with a customized module. Weekday, weekend and online batches are available. Request a callback for the exact duration and batch dates.

Do I need any prior experience or technical background?

No. The course starts from the basics, so students, freshers, professionals and business owners from any background can join.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes. We provide 100% placement assistance including resume building, mock interviews and interview opportunities with our hiring partners.

Which certifications will I get?

You'll receive the DIDM course certificate and be prepared for industry certifications such as Google Ads, Google Analytics and Meta Blueprint.

Can I attend a demo class before enrolling?

Absolutely. Book a free demo class using the form above and experience our teaching style first.

Is EMI available for the course fee?

Yes, easy EMI options are available. Speak to our counsellor for details on current offers.
